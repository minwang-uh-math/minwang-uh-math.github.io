My research develops reliable and effective methods at the intersection of artificial intelligence and computational mathematics. My goal is to transform predictive modeling of complex systems by combining AI with mathematical rigor. Integrating numerical analysis, scientific computing, and machine learning, my team tackles problems where conventional methods are prohibitively expensive and purely data-driven models unreliable. A defining feature of my program is its emphasis on methods that scale to real-world challenges, achieving orders-of-magnitude speedups and enabling industrial applications. Together, these advances expand what is computationally possible in fluid dynamics, energy and environmental systems, and finance.

<h2>Research keywords</h2>
<p>Scientific Machine Learning; Reduced-Order Modeling; Multiscale Methods; High-Dimensional PDEs; Flow and Transport in Engineering, Environmental, and Biological Systems; Mathematical Finance</p>
